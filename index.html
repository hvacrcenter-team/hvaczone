Indoor air quality

IAQ products we install & service

Clean air is half of comfort. These whole-home systems integrate with your HVAC equipment to filter, ventilate, and balance humidity — far beyond what a standard 1-inch filter can do. We specify, install, and service them all, with no single-brand bias.

Media air cleaners

High-MERV (MERV 13–16) media filtration built into your ductwork — captures dust, pollen, pet dander, and fine particles with minimal airflow loss.

UV-C germicidal lights

Ultraviolet light in the airstream and at the coil to reduce bacteria, viruses, and mold — and keep the evaporator coil clean for better efficiency.

Whole-home humidifiers

Bypass, fan-powered, and steam humidifiers that hold a healthy 30–50% relative humidity in winter — protecting sinuses, woodwork, and electronics.

Whole-home dehumidifiers

Tackle damp basements and humid summers, cut mold and dust-mite growth, and let you set the thermostat a few degrees higher while staying comfortable.

Ventilation (ERV / HRV)

Energy and heat recovery ventilators bring in fresh, filtered outdoor air and exhaust stale air while recovering the heating or cooling you already paid for.

Air purifiers & VOC control

Whole-home purifiers with carbon and photocatalytic stages that neutralize odors, smoke, and volatile organic compounds from cooking, cleaning, and furnishings.

UVGI in HVAC: How Ultraviolet Germicidal Irradiation Cleans Coils and Disinfects Air

UVGI uses UV-C light at 253.7 nm to damage DNA and RNA of microorganisms, preventing replication. ASHRAE recognizes it as the only technology for effective disinfection of air and surfaces in HVAC systems, with documented kill rates above 99% and coil-cleaning energy savings of 10-30%.

VRF Systems Explained: How Variable Refrigerant Flow Cuts Commercial HVAC Energy 30-50%

VRF systems use inverter-driven compressors and electronic expansion valves to serve multiple zones from one outdoor unit. DOE, ORNL, and GSA studies document 15-52% HVAC energy savings over conventional systems, with heat recovery enabling simultaneous heating and cooling.

Thermal Bridging & Continuous Insulation: Why Your Wall's R-Value Isn't What You Think

An R-13 wall only delivers R-9.4 in practice — thermal bridging through studs, steel, and concrete bypasses insulation. Continuous insulation fixes this. ASHRAE 90.1 requires it in most climate zones.
