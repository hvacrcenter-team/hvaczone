Trust & Standards

Editorial Policy

How the HVAC Zone Information Hub sources, writes, and fact-checks every article — and what we will and won't do.

Our mission

To be the most useful, trustworthy, and comprehensive HVAC information source on the internet — for homeowners, technicians, and builders. We aim to explain, compare, and inform. We do not aim to sell you a brand.

Brand-neutral by design

HVAC Zone Inc is a multi-brand dealer (Daikin/Goodman, Rheem/Ruud, Coleman/JCI, American Standard, Armstrong, Mitsubishi, Samsung, LG, Cooper & Hunter, Gree, TCI, Nortek family, Carrier/Bryant, and more). The Information Hub is not. Articles compare products and methods by objective criteria — efficiency, cost, durability, serviceability, and real-world performance — never by brand loyalty. When we mention a specific manufacturer or model, it is because it is relevant to the topic, not because we are paid or preferential. Naming a product is not an endorsement.

Sourcing

Every factual claim is backed by at least one credible source. We prioritize, in order:

  • Primary / regulatory / standards bodies — EPA, DOE, ASHRAE, AHRI, ICC, OSHA.
  • Independent trade press and peer-reviewed research.
  • Manufacturer specifications — used only for product facts (ratings, dimensions), clearly labeled as manufacturer claims.

We do not treat contractor blogs or marketing pages as factual authority. Opinion is labeled as opinion.

Citations

Articles link to their sources inline, using the real URL of the primary document. If a claim cannot be sourced, it does not get published.

Structure of every article

To keep content genuinely useful and not SEO filler, each article includes:

  • What changed / what's new — the news hook or the core question.
  • Who it helps — which reader this is for.
  • The comparison or method — a table, checklist, diagram, or field-tech interpretation.
  • Limitations — what the data can't tell you.
  • "Not an endorsement" language where products are named.

Daily publishing

The Hub publishes new content daily, rotating across six categories: Equipment Reviews, Tools, Indoor Air Quality, Smart Thermostats, Building Science, and Product Trends — plus a weekly roundup. Articles are researched from current, independent web sources and written to the standards above.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction. Spot an error? Email info@hvaczonenj.com.

The disclaimer

Articles are informational and educational. They are not a substitute for a site-specific assessment by a qualified, licensed HVAC professional. Always follow manufacturer instructions and local codes. HVAC Zone Inc is a licensed NJ Master HVACR contractor; this content is provided as a public resource, not a solicitation.
